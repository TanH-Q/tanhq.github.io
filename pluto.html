Explore Pluto

A World Beyond Neptune

Pluto is a dwarf planet located in the Kuiper Belt, a region of icy objects beyond Neptune. It was discovered by Clyde Tombaugh in 1930 and reclassified as a dwarf planet by the International Astronomical Union in 2006.

Quick Facts

  • Pluto was discovered in 1930.
  • It takes about 248 Earth years to orbit the Sun.
  • Its surface contains frozen nitrogen, methane, and water ice.
  • Its largest moon is Charon.
  • It has a thin atmosphere that can freeze onto its surface.

A Changing Landscape

Images from the New Horizons mission revealed mountains, icy plains, and a heart-shaped region known as Tombaugh Regio. These discoveries showed that Pluto is a complex world with a surface that has changed over time.
