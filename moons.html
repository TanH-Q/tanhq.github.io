A SMALL PLANETARY FAMILY

Pluto's Moons

Five Known Moons

Pluto has five known natural satellites. Charon is the largest, while Styx, Nix, Kerberos, and Hydra are smaller.

Charon

Pluto's largest moon is so large relative to Pluto that the two are sometimes described as a double dwarf-planet system.

Nix and Hydra

These smaller moons were discovered in observations made with the Hubble Space Telescope.

Styx and Kerberos

These two small moons complete Pluto's known family of five natural satellites.
