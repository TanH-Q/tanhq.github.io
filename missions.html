The New Horizons Mission

A Historic Flyby

NASA launched New Horizons in 2006. On July 14, 2015, the spacecraft made the first close flyby of Pluto, returning detailed images and scientific measurements.

Why the Mission Matters

New Horizons transformed Pluto from a distant point of light into a world we could study in detail. Its findings helped scientists better understand Pluto and other icy bodies in the outer solar system.
